---
name: ErgoEnf product direction
description: Product language and interaction principles for future ErgoEnf work
---

The ErgoEnf experience should behave like a decision aid used between nursing-care tasks, not like a wellness app or an online course. Start with the next observable decision: approach the bed, adjust height, coordinate the team, or stop and ask for help. Keep progress secondary to practical repertoire.

**Why:** Generic motivational language, fictional social proof, oversized progress treatments, and vague labels made the original interface feel disconnected from a real hospital shift.

**How to apply:** Use direct Portuguese verbs and procedure-specific commands. Treat ebook photos as documentary or didactic evidence, preserve their natural proportions, and give diagrams their own readable frame instead of cropping them into decorative cards.
